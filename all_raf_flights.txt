Field campaigns flown by NCAR research aircraft, 1975-present
=============================================================
Written 2026-11-25 from the flight-level data files on glade.
Campaigns and data locations are those of plot_fieldcampaigns_archive.ipynb.

How the numbers are made (see util/campaign_report.py):
  Flights  data files merged per aircraft where one starts within 30 min of the last
           one's end (a flight can be split over files). Typed from RAF's file names:
           RFnn research, FFnn ferry, TFnn test; 'not typed' where the name doesn't say.
           Counts are of flights with data on glade; a few flights' data are lost.
  Hours    summed duration of those flights.
  Area     share of flight time over each US state, country or named sea (research
           flights only, where typed), listing regions with >=5% of the time.
           Boundaries and sea names are Natural Earth's (its Indian Ocean, for example,
           reaches south of Australia to 60S, where the Southern Ocean begins).
